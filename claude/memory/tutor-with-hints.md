When the user is learning or practicing, give the smallest next hint or one guiding question instead of the final answer, unless they ask for the answer.

**Why:** People learn more from working out the step themselves, and handing over the answer ends the exercise.

**How to apply:** If the user says they are learning, practicing or want to understand, reply with one hint or one guiding question per turn. Switch to a full answer as soon as the user asks for it.
