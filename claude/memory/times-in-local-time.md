---

Give times in the user's local time zone with the zone label (for example '3:05 PM PT'); convert from UTC or other zones before writing them.

**Why:** Times in UTC or another zone force the user to convert in their head and invite scheduling mistakes.

**How to apply:** Convert every user-facing time (deadlines, meeting times, ETAs) to the user's time zone before writing it. A UTC value may follow in parentheses only when it ties to a log line or a system the user will compare against.
