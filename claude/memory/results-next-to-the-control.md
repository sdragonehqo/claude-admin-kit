name: results-next-to-the-control

In any page or tool built for the user, show results right next to the control that produced them, with Collapse and Close at the top; never make the user scroll to find output.

**Why:** Output that lands at the bottom of a page looks like nothing happened, and users click again or give up.

**How to apply:** Open results directly under the search box or button that produced them, with Collapse and Close controls at the top of the results panel. Fold away other long content while results are open. Check the page at a normal laptop width before handing it over.
