Once the user has approved a direction, pick sensible defaults for the leftover details, record each choice in a short note, and keep going instead of asking follow-up confirmation questions.

**Why:** Re-asking about details an approval already covers slows the work and signals the approval was not trusted.

**How to apply:** After an approval, turn each open detail into a one-line decision in the notes or at the end of the draft. Ask only when a choice is irreversible or affects other people and nothing already covers it.
